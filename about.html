ABOUT LYRICS VIBES TEAM

Discover our history, purpose, vision, mission and the journey that continues to shape our creative community.

The Beginning — 03 April 2025

Lyrics Vibes Team was founded on 3rd April 2025 by Abba Master, with the support and contribution of SAS Smart Lyrics, Usama Lyrics, Siyama Lyrics, Sayyada Lyrics, Abba Miloniya, and Honourable Lyrics.

The idea began with a simple but meaningful purpose: to help people who wanted to learn Lyrics, improve their skills, and grow creatively together.

At the beginning, the team had 26 members who shared the same passion for Lyrics and creative learning.

Becoming a Team — 12 June 2025

On 12th June 2025, Lyrics Vibes Team officially developed from a learning platform into a structured creative team.

This became an important milestone in our journey. Members were not only learning Lyrics, but also sharing knowledge, supporting one another, participating in activities, and developing their creative abilities.

Our Growth

Since its establishment, Lyrics Vibes Team has continued to experience significant growth and development.

Our activities have expanded to include Lyrics Creation, Editing & Design, Tutorials, Competitions, Events and Creative Skill Development.

Through teamwork, dedication, learning and continuous improvement, the team continues to create opportunities for members to develop and showcase their talents.
