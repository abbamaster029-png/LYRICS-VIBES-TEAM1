🎯 How To Participate

Participants created Lyrics Videos according to the competition requirements and submitted their work within the announced period. The final table above records the official points and ranking.

⭐ Judging Criteria

✨ Text Animation

Quality and creativity of lyric text animation.

🎨 Text Colors

Effective use of text colours and visual presentation.

🖼️ Background

Suitability and creativity of the background.

📝 Word-by-Word Lyrics

Accuracy and timing of lyrics presentation.

💡 Creativity

Original ideas and creative execution.

🎞️ Overall Editing

Overall quality, editing and final presentation.

📜 Competition Rules

  • Participants submitted their work within the announced deadline.
  • All competition requirements and guidelines were to be followed.
  • The submitted work was required to be created by the participant.
  • Participants were expected to respect the competition instructions provided by the team.
  • The competition officially ended on 21 June 2026.
