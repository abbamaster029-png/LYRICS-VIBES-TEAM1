LVT Lyrics Competition

Lyrics Video Competition

About The Competition

LVT Lyrics Competition is a creative Lyrics Video competition organized by Lyrics Vibes Team to encourage creativity, teamwork and video editing skills.

👥 Eligibility

The competition is open to Lyrics Vibes Team members only.

🆓 Entry

Participation is completely free. No registration fee is required.

📅 Schedule

The competition is held every two months.

🎬 Competition Type

Participants compete by creating creative Lyrics Videos.

🎯 How To Participate

Members can participate by creating a Lyrics Video according to the competition requirements and submitting their work within the announced deadline. Participants are encouraged to contribute positively to the team and showcase their creativity.

⭐ Judging Criteria

✨ Text Animation
🎨 Text Colors
🖼️ Background
📝 Word-by-Word Lyrics
💡 Creativity
🎞️ Overall Editing

📤 Submission

All competition entries must be submitted within the announced submission period. Late submissions may not be accepted.

🏅 Prizes & Recognition

Winners will receive a Certificate of Achievement along with other prizes and recognition prepared for the competition.

📜 Certificate

Certificate images will be added here later.

📜 Competition Rules

  • Submit your work within the announced deadline.
  • Follow all competition requirements and guidelines.
  • The submitted work should be created by the participant.
  • Participants should respect the competition instructions provided by the team.
  • Any additional rules will be announced when the competition begins.
